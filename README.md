# Luminara — LumiWeapons

Paper 1.21.11 plugin: legendary weapons forged at ritual altars, weapon fragments, and admin-run
events. The `resourcepack/` folder is the matching Luminara resource pack.

## Building

```
mvn package          # -> target/LumiWeapons-<version>.jar
```

Zip the *contents* of `resourcepack/` (so `pack.mcmeta` sits at the root of the zip) to get the
resource pack.

## Resource pack

Every weapon has a 3D model (inventory keeps the 2D sprite; hands, frames and the ground show the
model). Glowing parts are animated. The models are generated by the scripts in
`resourcepack/_source/` (`toonexport.py` rebuilds all of them and a Blockbench project for each);
held poses are solved against simulations of vanilla's first- and third-person item transforms,
so polearms are carried upright and the crossbow is held level. Thrown weapons (Glaive, Lost
Trident, Axe of Zeus) fly as their own models.

**Controls, the same for every weapon with an ultimate:** ability key = Ability 1, sneak + ability
key = Ability 2, **double-tap the ability key** = the ultimate, once its meter is full
(`ultimates.double-tap-millis`, 400 by default).

**Ultimates** (Talaria, Twin Requiem, Crescent, Oculus, Horizon and the twelve below): hitting
players and using the weapon's abilities fill a meter on the action bar; double-tap fires the
ultimate when it is full. It drains slowly out of combat. Every ultimate telegraphs itself (a wind-up, a circle on
the ground, a glow) so it can be escaped or blocked:
- **Blade of Talaria:** hold right-click and the blade blocks like a shield, so a totem can stay in
  the off-hand; its shield floats around you. Axes disable it for the normal 5s, the legendary axes
  no longer. Blocking and hitting fill **Divine Ascension**: rise in light, untouchable for 4s.
- **Twin Requiem:** hits in a row build Momentum (a miss or a pause resets it); full Momentum makes
  the next special reach further. **Cross Reap** sweeps forward, then all round you. **Final
  Harvest** winds up for 1.5s, then a huge circular swing that slows.
- **Crescent:** **Harvest Mark** cuts and marks the enemy in front; three hits detonate the mark.
  **Moonlit Execution**: three quick slashes, then a final strike that grows with each slash that
  landed. Nothing teleports or chases.
- **Oculus:** **Watcher** names the nearest enemy you can see (line of sight only). **Eye Beam**
  charges briefly, then fires down your aim and slows. **Judgement**: a giant eye marks the ground
  where you look, then strikes it after 2.5s. Blocks are never harmed.
- **Horizon:** **True Aim**: a full draw held still focuses the shot (faster, then a small bonus;
  never homing). **Piercing Arrow** passes through one enemy but not a raised shield. **Heaven's
  Fall** marks where it lands, then falls from the sky; cover blocks it.

Their models are in the resource pack: Talaria, Twin Requiem, Crescent and Oculus on the netherite
sword (custom_model_data 17-20), Horizon on the bow (21, with its draw states), and Talaria's
floating shield on the shield (22).

**Lore** is in `lore.yml`, laid out the same way for every weapon:
- a flavour line and the power tier
- **✦ Passive**, marked when it also works from the off-hand
- **✦ Ability**
- one cooldown line

Lines are written as `what | result`. The numbers come from the weapon's abilities in `config.yml`
(`{s:dash-cooldown}` etc.), so lore and balance never drift apart. Weapons players already own
update when either file changes.

**Off-hand passives:** a legendary in the off-hand gives its passives (not its abilities), so you
can combine two weapons' passives. This works for the Scythe of Diablo, Axe of Zeus, Hermes's Blade,
Chione's Blade, Titan's Axe, Blade of Diminution, and three of the new ones: the **Blade of
Talaria** (in the off-hand it is your shield: any sword in the main hand, Talaria blocks and its
shield floats around you), **Oculus** (Watcher still names who it sees) and **Horizon** (True Aim
focuses any bow you draw), and the **Earthshaker Hammer** (Heavy Impact), **Abyssal Trident** (Deep
Pressure) and **Glacial Glaive** (Frostbite) on whatever you hit with. Every ultimate also works with `/charge` for testing. `passives.off-hand-strength` sets how strong
they are: `FULL`, or `REDUCED` (×`reduced-multiplier`).

**Cooldowns:** each legendary greys out on its own when an ability is on cooldown, not every item
of the same type. The action bar shows a filling bar and the seconds left, then "✔ ready". It also
shows charges and meters, the off-hand weapon whose passives are active, your Veil disguise, and
your bounty target during a Bounty Hunt. The Lost Trident has a throw cooldown (`throw-cooldown`).

Abilities have short animations (ground rings, debris, ice spikes, a slash) built from display
blocks: under a second, at ground level or around the caster, hidden for `/lwfx off`, and switched
off entirely with `effects.animations: false`. Every ability also has one textured effect from the resource pack (`effects.sprites`), in the
weapon's colours. Examples: SuperNova's rune circle, gathering light, flash and shockwave; the
Eclipse split's eclipse and light/dark slashes; slashes for the Scythe's backstab and finisher, the
Glaive and Zeus throws, Ice Wave and the Hermes and Luminosity dashes; bursts for freezes, shrinks,
hooks, the Zeus impact, the Hellstorm volley and the Veil; ground rings for the Water Wall, the
trident pulse, the Slam and Hellstorm's explosions. They are short, hidden for `/lwfx minimal` and
`off`, and never shown to someone a burst lands on. The textures are generated by
`resourcepack/_source/vfx_sprites.py`. Kill progress (Sword of Eclipse, Hermes's Blade)
survives logging out; only dying resets it. The Ender Glaive and the Axe of Zeus throw an echo of
themselves and stay in your hand, so you can keep swinging them (the throw cooldown limits it).
The Lost Trident is thrown for real and comes back on loyalty.

## Amethyst tools

DonutSMP-style tools (`/lwtest give AMETHYST_PICKAXE|AMETHYST_SHOVEL|AMETHYST_AXE`, the `/gui`
menu, or the tokens in any loot/reward list — they are in the late supply drop by default):

* **Pickaxe / Shovel** — break a 3x3 facing you (flat when looking up or down).
* **Axe** — fells the whole tree and clears its natural leaves; only real trees, so log builds are
  safe. Silk Touch.

Sneak for a single block. Extra blocks respect claims/regions, never touch containers, spawners or
anything harder than `tools.max-hardness`, and wear the tool (Unbreaking applies).

## Getting legendaries

* **Altar kinds** (`ritual.altar-mode`): `CRAFTED`, `ANCIENT` or `BOTH`.
  - **Ancient Altars** can't be crafted. Admins hide a few around the map:
    - with the Ancient Altar item, or `/lwevent altar place`
    - `/lwevent altar scatter [count] [radius]` puts them at random around spawn
    - `ritual.ancient-altars.max` (3) caps how many exist
  - They can't be broken, show a label up close, and the first player to use each one is announced.
  - In `ANCIENT` mode the altar recipe is off and crafted altars go dormant.
* **Crafting times:**
  - Rituals can only be started inside `ritual.windows`. By default that's every day from 4 PM to
    midnight Central Time (`ritual.timezone`); admins can start one any time.
  - When the window opens everyone is told the altars have awakened, and again when they fall
    quiet (`ritual.windows.announce`). Outside the window altars sleep (no crystal, a wisp of ash),
    and an Ancient Altar's label says "Awake · until 9:00 PM CDT" or "Asleep · wakes 4:00 PM CDT".
  - `/lw ritualclock` shows the server's ritual clock, the windows, whether they're open, and any
    line in `ritual.windows.times` it can't read.
  - Ritual messages show the clock time a ritual finishes ("done at 8:42 PM CDT").
  - `weapons.<weapon>.ritual-time` gives one weapon its own ritual length.
* **Ritual Altar** — crafted (obsidian/diamonds/nether star) or `/giveritualblock`. Right-click it
  for a 5-slot ritual menu that previews the weapon the ingredients will forge. A running ritual
  shows every player a boss bar with its coordinates, distance and direction — or, when you are in
  the Overworld and it is in the Nether (or the other way round), the coordinates converted to your
  dimension. Breaking the altar ends the ritual and spills the ingredients.

  The altar has its own model in the resource pack (basalt, gold trim, glowing amethyst runes).
  Underneath it is an empty spawner: it mines like an enchanting table, pistons cannot move it, and
  explosions and withers leave it alone. Old enchanting-table altars convert when their chunk loads;
  `ritual.custom-altar: false` keeps plain enchanting tables. While a ritual runs, two rune circles
  in the weapon's colour turn on the ground. Four crystal pylons light their beams into the weapon
  one per quarter of the time, so progress reads from a distance. A beam climbs into the sky over the
  weapon. In the last two seconds the pylons fly into the weapon, then a flash and a shockwave. The
  models come from `resourcepack/_source/altar.py`; Blockbench copies are in `resourcepack/_source/blockbench/`.
* **Weapon Fragments** (Tier I–IV) — dropped by bosses (`fragments.mob-drops`), found in supply
  drops, won in events. 4 of a tier fuse into one of the next tier at the altar.
* **Luminosity Fragment** — only from events. Luminosity's recipe needs 3.
* `acquisition:` in the config switches ritual crafting, fragment requirements, one-per-server
  legendaries and fusion on or off.

## Events

The **Event Director** (`director` in the config, on by default) runs events by itself: each
time window picks one event at random from its weighted list and a random time inside the window,
then counts down in chat (10, 5, 1 minutes). Players know *something* happens in the evening, not
what or exactly when. It waits for `director.min-players`. Admins can still start anything by hand.

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/lwevent director on\|off\|status\|skip\|run <event>` | Control the Director; `run` starts e.g. `fallenstar`, `bloodmoon`, `supplydrop:late` now. |
| `/lwevent fallenstar start [world]` | A meteor crashes at a random spot on natural ground. Its location is revealed in stages (biome, then distance from spawn, then coordinates). The crater holds the sealed **Star Core**; the first players to arrive wake waves of guardians. Clearing the last wave frees the core as a carry objective. Guardian fighters are paid by contribution. The crater heals a few minutes later (only blocks nobody changed). |
| `/lwevent bloodmoon start [world]` | Rises at the next nightfall, sets at dawn: more and stronger monsters, rare glowing Blood-Touched elites, double drops and XP, a red screen-edge tint, no sleeping, and killing a player puts a token bounty on your head until dawn (the killer collects it; survive and it's yours). |

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/lwevent bounty start [min]` | Everyone gets a target, tracked on the action bar (distance + arrow, or the target's dimension). Killing your target scores on a sidebar leaderboard; the top hunter wins a Luminosity Fragment. |
| `/lwevent koth marker` → place it → `/lwevent koth start [min]` | King of the Hill with points: holding the hill alone, hold streaks, captures and hill kills all score (`events.koth.points`). First to `win-points` wins, else the top score when time runs out. A glowing ring on the ground marks the hill (red while contested) with a light column over the centre, and a floating leaderboard sits above it. Winner gets a Luminosity Fragment. |
| `/lwevent supplydrop <early\|mid\|late> [count] [world]` | Crates fall at random safe spots inside the world border, with a beam until claimed. Loot tables per tier are in the config. |
| `/lwevent mostwanted set <name>` → `confirm` | The named player is anonymous from the moment they join (no join message, neutral name, default skin). After an admin confirms, they get 20 hearts and Speed II and their rough position is broadcast regularly. On death: a blast throws players back, a loot chest appears with a Luminosity Fragment in the centre, and they are banned (`events.most-wanted.ban-on-death`). |
| `/chosen pick \| set <player> \| clear` or `/lwevent chosen ...` (or Admin panel > Events > The Chosen) | **The Chosen:** a random player who owns no legendary gets a halo of light. When they fight someone carrying a legendary (in hand or anywhere in their inventory), the Chosen's own counterpart of it becomes a copy of that legendary: a legendary sword turns their diamond or netherite sword, a legendary axe their axe, the Lost Trident a trident, Horizon a bow, Hellstorm a crossbow, Luminosity a mace, the Veil a book, the Tether Chain a lead (Oculus is a staff on the netherite sword, so a sword answers it; `events.chosen.counterparts`; the one in hand first, else the first one carried). That item becomes a copy of the opponent's legendary (an "echo") until the fight ends: 15 seconds without a hit between them, too far apart, a death or a logout. If someone with a different legendary joins the fight, the Chosen is offered **[Switch]** or **[Keep]** in chat for 10 seconds (`events.chosen.switch-offer`); the same legendary just carries on. The echo can't be dropped, stored or kept, and always turns back into the weapon it came from. If the Chosen takes up a real legendary, the role passes to someone at random who has none (`events.chosen.pass-on-legendary`). If the Chosen is killed by a player without a legendary, the killer becomes the Chosen; killed by a legendary holder, it passes to someone at random (`events.chosen.pass-on-death`). Settings: `events.chosen`. |
| `/lwevent outpost ...` | Outposts. |
| `/lwevent draft start [captains...]` (or Admin panel > Events > Team Draft) | The captain draft. Captains pick players in snake order on the draft board (`/draft`), with a timer on each pick. `/lwevent team add\|remove\|create\|disband\|list` manages teams by hand. |
| `/lwevent altar place\|scatter\|remove\|list` | Ancient Altars. |
| `/lwevent boss arena <boss> [radius]\|start [boss]\|stop\|status` | Bosses: hollow_warden, wither_king, leviathan, ender_colossus. |
| `/lwevent trader spawn\|remove\|list` | Place a Token Trader where you stand (facing you), remove the one next to you, or list them. |
| `/lwevent status`, `/lwevent stopall` | Overview of everything; stop bounty, KotH, Fallen Star and Blood Moon without prizes. |

Winners who are offline when an event ends get their prize the next time they join.

### Bosses

Set an arena per boss (`/lwevent boss arena <boss> [radius]`, standing at its centre), then
`/lwevent boss start <boss>` or let the Director run one. Only one boss fights at a time.

* **Hollow Warden** (deep dark) — coordination. Hunts the *loudest* player (sprinting, jumping and
  hitting are loud, sneaking is quiet; your noise is on the action bar). Marked sonic lines you
  sidestep; sculk spikes erupt under the loudest players; from 66% it roars and a shockwave rolls
  out along the ground, so jump it. At 66% and 33% it buries itself in sculk and only breaks out
  when every sculk node is charged at the same time; tendrils seize players until someone cuts
  them free; darkness in the last phase. It **learns**: one kind of damage doing most of the work
  gets resisted for a while.
* **Wither King** — raid boss, and it hurts. Four Soul Anchors shield it, each broken differently:
  North melee; South **lure its skulls onto it** (stand by it, draw aimed skulls, dodge at the
  last moment); East stand on it; West kill its summons. Leading skull volleys from the start, a
  Soul Beam that locks its aim and then fires through armour, and Wither on every hit. The arena
  withers and a Wither Nova punishes crowding it from 70%; the anchors return with skull rain at
  40%; at 15% the King lands, slams, summons and leaps at whoever is furthest away.
* **Leviathan** (open water) — movement. Mostly submerged; it surfaces in a rhythm and takes extra
  damage while up. Drags people under, water bolts, geysers under everyone, whirlpools, a tidal
  wave across the arena from 60%, sinks boats, summons drowned and guardians, floods the dry
  ground late. The Lost Trident of Atlantis hits it 75% harder.
* **Ender Colossus** — chaos. Every trick has a tell: tethered swaps, banishing its top damage
  dealer, pulling the farthest player in, void rifts that drag in and implode, and from 60% a
  stomp out of the sky onto someone (watch for the shadow), decoys (the real one drips portal
  particles) and a final position scramble.

Shared rules: health scales with the number of fighters (also when more arrive), only hits from
inside the arena and on foot count, the boss is leashed and never breaks blocks, arena changes heal
afterwards, and **a player who dies in the arena feeds the boss** (it heals and hits harder), so
betraying people mid-fight can cost everyone the kill. On death everyone is paid by contribution and
**the Boss Heart drops**. Custom models: every boss (and the Token Trader) has a Blockbench
template already wired into the pack and the config. Model over it, export, and set
`model.enabled: true`. See [docs/BOSS_MODELS.md](docs/BOSS_MODELS.md).

### Shared fights, carry objectives, outposts and teams

* **Contribution** — shared fights (Fallen Star guardians now, bosses next) score everyone on
  damage (60%), mechanics (20%) and support/objectives (20%). Everyone above 3% gets a reward;
  the top three and the top contributor get more. Prizes go to the **reward chest** (`/rewards`),
  so nothing is lost to a full inventory or being offline. Players can only take items out.
* **Carry objectives** — the **Boss Heart** and the **Star Core** drop on the ground. Whoever
  holds one for 45-60 seconds claims the big reward. The carrier glows through walls, has it
  floating over their head, is on everyone's boss bar and has their position announced; anyone
  can kill them and take it. No pearls, chorus fruit, elytra, portals or teleport commands while
  carrying. Logging out drops it.
* **Outposts** — `/lwevent outpost create <name> <plains|nether|end|ancient> [radius]`. Stand in
  one alone (you + players you mutually trust count as one side) to capture it; someone else's is
  neutralised first. Owners earn a small hourly income (tokens + resources, into `/rewards`) while
  online, at most 2 outposts each. `/outposts` lists them.
* **Luminara Tokens** — event currency from outposts, relics, Fallen Stars and Blood Moon elites.
  Trade them with a **Token Trader**: a villager an admin places with `/lwevent trader spawn`
  (consumables, materials, fragments only; `/tokenshop` shows where traders stand). Traders never
  move, cannot be hurt, and nobody but ops can build, dig, pour or burn around them.
* **Teams** — made in a **captain draft**:
  - An admin chooses the captains, and anyone who should sit out.
  - Captains take turns picking from the pool on the draft board (`/draft`), in snake or round
    order. A boss bar shows each pick's timer. When the time runs out, a random player is picked
    (or the pick passes, with auto-pick off).
  - Everything about the draft is under `teams.draft` and can be set on the draft setup page:
    pick time, warning, order, captain order, max team size, team names, colours, boss bar,
    titles, sounds, whether old teams are replaced, and what a cancel does.
  - Teammates are allies. `teams.allies` picks which parts apply: abilities skip them, friendly
    fire, shared outposts, and bounty hunts never targeting a teammate.
  - Names are in the team colour in the tab list and over heads, with ♛ for the captain and ✦ for
    members (`teams.nametags`: symbols, their colours, an optional `[Team]`, or off entirely). A
    Veil disguise copies its target's look and its name tag follows the player smoothly.
  - Team chat: `/tc <message>`, or `/team chat` to make it the default (`teams.chat`).
  - `/team` opens your team: members, distance, team chat. Captains can rename it, and kick when
    `teams.captains.can-kick` is on. `/team leave` (when `teams.allow-leave` is on).
  - Admins manage every team in **Admin Panel > Teams & Draft**: rename, recolour, add or remove
    players, change the captain, disband.

## Commands

**`/lumi`** (also `/luminara`) opens the Luminara menu:
- recipes (click a weapon for its ingredients, ritual and off-hand passives)
- your team and the draft board
- the reward chest
- your settings (ability key, effect level)
- trusted players
- what's on in the world, and how many Ancient Altars have been found

The hub shows the legendaries in your hands and lights up while an event is running. Admins also get the **Admin Panel** (`/lw` or `/lumi admin`), with:
- **Server Settings:** every config.yml setting in game, with the comments from the file as descriptions.
  - Booleans toggle on click. Numbers go up and down with left and right click (shift for ten
    steps, Q to type a value). Text is typed in chat.
  - A ✎ marks a setting changed from the default; Ctrl+Q on it puts the default back.
  - Changes save to config.yml and apply straight away, like `/lw reload`. A few, such as crafting
    recipes, need a restart. Saving keeps the file's comments but normalises its spacing.
- **Events and Bosses:** start or stop each event, run the Event Director, summon bosses and set their arenas.
- **Teams & Draft:** every team, the draft setup, and making teams by hand.
- **Give Items** (shift-click for a stack) **and Testing Tools.**

## Protection and anti-abuse

* Abilities respect the world PvP setting, WorldGuard's `pvp` flag and Towny PvP (soft
  dependencies, detected automatically). Block-changing abilities check build permission.
* Charged ultimates (Luminosity, Titan's Axe) only charge from player hits, are throttled per
  victim and drain out of combat. Cooldowns persist through death and relogging.
* Veil chat is logged with the real sender's name and UUID to the console and `veil-chat.log`.

## Upgrading from LegendaryWeapons (1.2) to LumiWeapons (1.3)

1. Stop the server.
2. Delete the old `LegendaryWeapons-*.jar` from `plugins/` and put in `LumiWeapons-1.3.0.jar`.
   With both jars installed, both plugins would run at once.
3. Start the server. `plugins/LegendaryWeapons/` is moved to `plugins/LumiWeapons/` on the first
   start, so your settings, altars, rituals, teams, reward chests and trader spots all carry over.

Nothing in the world changes: weapons, altars, Token Traders and other plugin items keep working,
because their tags keep the old `legendaryweapons` namespace. Permission groups that grant
`legendaryweapons.admin` still work. The chat prefix changes to `[LumiWeapons]`, unless you set your
own. The commands stay the same (`/lw`, `/lwevent`, `/lumi`, ...).

## Upgrading from 1.1

The old `config.yml` is saved as `config-v1-backup.yml` and replaced with the new defaults on the
first start (many balance values changed). Re-apply custom edits from the backup. Ritual altars and
running rituals from 1.1 are carried over; altars placed in 1.1 that were lost on restart have to
be placed again.
