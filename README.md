# Luminara — LegendaryWeapons

Paper 1.21.11 plugin: legendary weapons forged at ritual altars, weapon fragments, and admin-run
events. The `resourcepack/` folder is the matching Luminara resource pack.

## Building

```
mvn package          # -> target/LegendaryWeapons-<version>.jar
```

Zip the *contents* of `resourcepack/` (so `pack.mcmeta` sits at the root of the zip) to get the
resource pack.

## Resource pack

Every weapon has a 3D model (inventory keeps the 2D sprite; hands, frames and the ground show the
model). Glowing parts are animated. The models are generated by the scripts in
`resourcepack/_source/` (`toonexport.py` rebuilds all of them and a Blockbench project for each);
held poses are solved against simulations of vanilla's first- and third-person item transforms,
so polearms are carried upright and the crossbow is held level. Thrown weapons (Glaive, Lost
Trident, Axe of Zeus) fly as their own models.

## Weapons

| Tier | Weapon | Ability |
|---|---|---|
| I | Ender Glaive, Hermes's Blade | spear throw / pearl; dash through players |
| II | Chione's Blade, Lost Trident, Blade of Diminution, **Tether Chain** | freeze + Ice Wave; water wall; shrink yourself; hook and pull |
| III | Scythe of Diablo, Axe of Zeus, Hellstorm, Titan's Axe, Veil of Mimicry | combos/backstab; throw + stun; volley; armour-breaking Slam; disguise |
| IV | Sword of Eclipse, Luminosity | kill-scaling split blades; dash charges + SuperNova |

Every item's lore lists its ability numbers and cooldown. Lore reads the numbers from
`config.yml` (`{s:dash-cooldown}` etc.), and weapons already in players' inventories are updated
when the config changes.

The action bar shows the held weapon's cooldowns, charges and meters, your Veil disguise, and your
bounty target during a Bounty Hunt.

Abilities have short animations (ground rings, debris, ice spikes, a slash) built from display
blocks: under a second, at ground level or around the caster, hidden for `/lwfx off`, and switched
off entirely with `effects.animations: false`. A few signature moments also use textured effects from
the resource pack (`effects.sprites`): SuperNova's rune circle, gathering light, flash and
shockwave; the Eclipse split's eclipse and alternating light/dark slashes; the Scythe's combo
finisher; Titan's Axe Slam cracks. These are hidden for `/lwfx minimal` and `off`. The textures are
generated by `resourcepack/_source/vfx_sprites.py`. Kill progress (Sword of Eclipse, Hermes's Blade)
survives logging out; only dying resets it. A thrown Glaive or Axe of Zeus is never lost: if its
owner logs out or the server stops mid-flight, it is handed back.

## Amethyst tools

DonutSMP-style tools (`/lwtest give AMETHYST_PICKAXE|AMETHYST_SHOVEL|AMETHYST_AXE`, the `/gui`
menu, or the tokens in any loot/reward list — they are in the late supply drop by default):

* **Pickaxe / Shovel** — break a 3x3 facing you (flat when looking up or down).
* **Axe** — fells the whole tree and clears its natural leaves; only real trees, so log builds are
  safe. Silk Touch.

Sneak for a single block. Extra blocks respect claims/regions, never touch containers, spawners or
anything harder than `tools.max-hardness`, and wear the tool (Unbreaking applies).

## Getting legendaries

* **Ritual Altar** — crafted (obsidian/diamonds/nether star) or `/giveritualblock`. Right-click it
  for a 5-slot ritual menu that previews the weapon the ingredients will forge. A running ritual
  shows every player a boss bar with its coordinates, distance and direction — or, when you are in
  the Overworld and it is in the Nether (or the other way round), the coordinates converted to your
  dimension. Breaking the altar ends the ritual and spills the ingredients.
* **Weapon Fragments** (Tier I–IV) — dropped by bosses (`fragments.mob-drops`), found in supply
  drops, won in events. 4 of a tier fuse into one of the next tier at the altar.
* **Luminosity Fragment** — only from events. Luminosity's recipe needs 3.
* `acquisition:` in the config switches ritual crafting, fragment requirements, one-per-server
  legendaries and fusion on or off.

## Events

The **Event Director** (`director` in the config, on by default) runs events by itself: each
time window picks one event at random from its weighted list and a random time inside the window,
then counts down in chat (10, 5, 1 minutes). Players know *something* happens in the evening, not
what or exactly when. It waits for `director.min-players`. Admins can still start anything by hand.

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/lwevent director on\|off\|status\|skip\|run <event>` | Control the Director; `run` starts e.g. `fallenstar`, `bloodmoon`, `supplydrop:late` now. |
| `/lwevent fallenstar start [world]` | A meteor crashes at a random spot on natural ground. Its location is revealed in stages (biome, then distance from spawn, then coordinates). The crater holds the sealed **Star Core**; the first players to arrive wake waves of guardians. Clearing the last wave frees the core as a carry objective. Guardian fighters are paid by contribution. The crater heals a few minutes later (only blocks nobody changed). |
| `/lwevent bloodmoon start [world]` | Rises at the next nightfall, sets at dawn: more and stronger monsters, rare glowing Blood-Touched elites, double drops and XP, a red screen-edge tint, no sleeping, and killing a player puts a Renown bounty on your head until dawn. |

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/lwevent bounty start [min]` | Everyone gets a target, tracked on the action bar (distance + arrow, or the target's dimension). Killing your target scores on a sidebar leaderboard; the top hunter wins a Luminosity Fragment. |
| `/lwevent koth marker` → place it → `/lwevent koth start [min]` | King of the Hill with points: holding the hill alone, hold streaks, captures and hill kills all score (`events.koth.points`). First to `win-points` wins, else the top score when time runs out. A glowing ring on the ground marks the hill (red while contested) with a light column over the centre, and a floating leaderboard sits above it. Winner gets a Luminosity Fragment. |
| `/lwevent supplydrop <early\|mid\|late> [count] [world]` | Crates fall at random safe spots inside the world border, with a beam until claimed. Loot tables per tier are in the config. |
| `/lwevent mostwanted set <name>` → `confirm` | The named player is anonymous from the moment they join (no join message, neutral name, default skin). After an admin confirms, they get 20 hearts and Speed II and their rough position is broadcast regularly. On death: a blast throws players back, a loot chest appears with a Luminosity Fragment in the centre, and they are banned (`events.most-wanted.ban-on-death`). |
| `/lwevent outpost ...`, `/lwevent season status\|reset confirm\|give <player> <n>` | Outposts and the season. |
| `/lwevent boss arena <boss> [radius]\|start [boss]\|stop\|status` | Bosses: hollow_warden, wither_king, leviathan, ender_colossus. |
| `/lwevent status`, `/lwevent stopall` | Overview of everything; stop bounty, KotH, Fallen Star and Blood Moon without prizes. |

Winners who are offline when an event ends get their prize the next time they join.

### Bosses

Set an arena per boss (`/lwevent boss arena <boss> [radius]`, standing at its centre), then
`/lwevent boss start <boss>` or let the Director run one. Only one boss fights at a time.

* **Hollow Warden** (deep dark) — coordination. Hunts the *loudest* player (sprinting, jumping and
  hitting are loud, sneaking is quiet; your noise is on the action bar). Marked sonic lines you
  sidestep; at 66% and 33% it buries itself in sculk and only breaks out when every sculk node is
  charged at the same time; tendrils seize players until someone cuts them free; darkness in the
  last phase. It **learns**: one kind of damage doing most of the work gets resisted for a while.
* **Wither King** — raid boss. Four Soul Anchors shield it, each broken differently (North melee,
  South ranged, East stand on it, West kill its summons). The arena withers in phase 2, the
  anchors return with skull rain in phase 3, and in phase 4 the King lands and fights on foot.
* **Leviathan** (open water) — movement. Mostly submerged; it surfaces in a rhythm and takes extra
  damage while up. Drags people under, water bolts, whirlpools, sinks boats, summons drowned and
  guardians, floods the dry ground late. The Lost Trident of Atlantis hits it 75% harder.
* **Ender Colossus** — chaos. Every trick has a tell: tethered swaps, banishing its top damage
  dealer, pulling the farthest player in, decoys (the real one drips portal particles) and a final
  position scramble.

Shared rules: health scales with the number of fighters (also when more arrive), only hits from
inside the arena and on foot count, the boss is leashed and never breaks blocks, arena changes heal
afterwards, and **a player who dies in the arena feeds the boss** (it heals and hits harder), so
betraying people mid-fight can cost everyone the kill. On death everyone is paid by contribution and
**the Boss Heart drops**. Custom models: see [docs/BOSS_MODELS.md](docs/BOSS_MODELS.md).

### Shared fights, carry objectives, outposts and seasons

* **Contribution** — shared fights (Fallen Star guardians now, bosses next) score everyone on
  damage (60%), mechanics (20%) and support/objectives (20%). Everyone above 3% gets a reward;
  the top three and the top contributor get more. Prizes go to the **reward chest** (`/rewards`),
  so nothing is lost to a full inventory or being offline. Players can only take items out.
* **Carry objectives** — the **Boss Heart** and the **Star Core** drop on the ground. Whoever
  holds one for 45-60 seconds claims the big reward. The carrier glows through walls, has it
  floating over their head, is on everyone's boss bar and has their position announced; anyone
  can kill them and take it. No pearls, chorus fruit, elytra, portals or teleport commands while
  carrying. Logging out drops it.
* **Outposts** — `/lwevent outpost create <name> <plains|nether|end|ancient> [radius]`. Stand in
  one alone (you + players you mutually trust count as one side) to capture it; someone else's is
  neutralised first. Owners earn a small hourly income (tokens + resources, into `/rewards`) while
  online, at most 2 outposts each. `/outposts` lists them.
* **Luminara Tokens** — event currency from outposts, relics, Fallen Stars and Blood Moon elites.
  Spend them in `/tokenshop` (consumables, materials, fragments only).
* **Season Renown** — every event, objective and PvP kill earns Renown (`season.renown`).
  `/renown` shows your stats and ranks; `/renown top <renown|kills|deaths|boss|bounties|outposts|hearts|stars>`
  shows a board. `/lwevent season reset confirm` ends the season, archives it, and pays the top
  three. The same killer/victim pair only counts once per 10 minutes.

## Protection and anti-abuse

* Abilities respect the world PvP setting, WorldGuard's `pvp` flag and Towny PvP (soft
  dependencies, detected automatically). Block-changing abilities check build permission.
* Charged ultimates (Luminosity, Titan's Axe) only charge from player hits, are throttled per
  victim and drain out of combat. Cooldowns persist through death and relogging.
* Veil chat is logged with the real sender's name and UUID to the console and `veil-chat.log`.

## Upgrading from 1.1

The old `config.yml` is saved as `config-v1-backup.yml` and replaced with the new defaults on the
first start (many balance values changed). Re-apply custom edits from the backup. Ritual altars and
running rituals from 1.1 are carried over; altars placed in 1.1 that were lost on restart have to
be placed again.
